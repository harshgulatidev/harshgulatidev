# Small business website launch checklist

By [Harsh Gulati / WebPerky](https://webperky.com/) · Updated 27 November 2026

A practical handover checklist for a brochure site, business catalogue or enquiry landing page. Mark items only after checking the live site. Use N/A with a reason where a feature does not apply.

## 1. Make the next step obvious

- [ ] State what the business offers, who it serves and its location or service area near the top of the homepage.
- [ ] Provide one clear primary action: enquire, request a quote, book or browse.
- [ ] Check every phone, email, WhatsApp and map link on a real phone.
- [ ] Keep business name, address and contact details consistent across the site and public business profiles.
- [ ] Confirm prices, availability, opening hours and delivery information with the business owner.

## 2. Test mobile and keyboard use

- [ ] Check narrow and wide screens for clipped text, sideways scrolling and overlapping controls.
- [ ] Open and close the mobile menu, including by keyboard.
- [ ] Navigate with Tab and Shift+Tab; verify visible focus and a sensible order.
- [ ] Give form fields visible labels and images meaningful alternative text where needed.
- [ ] Check text and button contrast; do not rely on colour alone to explain errors.
- [ ] Make videos controllable and avoid unexpected sound.

## 3. Verify the enquiry flow end to end

- [ ] Send a labelled test enquiry with an address you control. Verify receipt in the intended mailbox or CRM.
- [ ] Try empty, invalid and very long inputs. Explain errors near the relevant fields.
- [ ] Prevent repeated submissions while a request is processing.
- [ ] Display an accurate success or failure message; never show success when delivery failed.
- [ ] Validate untrusted input on the server and protect public forms against abuse.
- [ ] Collect only the details needed to respond; explain their purpose.

## 4. Check content and search basics

- [ ] Replace placeholder copy, demo contacts, broken images and outdated offers.
- [ ] Give each important page a descriptive title, main heading and useful summary.
- [ ] Link key pages through ordinary navigation and check that links reach the intended destination.
- [ ] Confirm that public pages are not accidentally blocked by robots settings or noindex.
- [ ] Check the preferred domain, HTTPS redirects and canonical URLs.
- [ ] Provide a sitemap when appropriate and verify site ownership in the relevant search tools.
- [ ] Update the website link on existing business profiles after owner approval. Submission does not guarantee indexing or ranking.

## 5. Keep delivery fast and reliable

- [ ] Resize and compress images for their displayed size. Load below-the-fold media when needed.
- [ ] Test the homepage and an important inner page over a slower mobile connection.
- [ ] Check browser console errors and missing requests during core tasks.
- [ ] Verify HTTPS and that embedded resources load without mixed-content errors.
- [ ] Keep credentials and private customer data out of client-side code and public repositories.
- [ ] Test the backup and restoration procedure before a significant launch or migration.

## 6. Hand over ownership clearly

- [ ] Record who controls the domain, hosting, repository and business mailboxes.
- [ ] Transfer access through the service permission tools; do not publish passwords in a handover document.
- [ ] Document content editing steps, renewal dates and the support scope.
- [ ] Keep analytics and marketing tools limited to what the business actually needs, with appropriate consent handling.
- [ ] Record known limitations and obtain a final content and enquiry-flow review from the owner.
